Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel held"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600): a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it.

## The entry

> My daughter fell asleep
> in my arms on the train.
> Her cheek warm
> against my chest.
> The carriage went quiet
> around us.
> I held her
> so she could sleep.
> She held me
> so I could stay.

One concrete scene, short lines, nothing to prove — a father held by the
child he is holding.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes the text above,
byte for byte, to `/output/entry.txt` as UTF-8 (176 characters).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
